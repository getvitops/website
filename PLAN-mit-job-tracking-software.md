### 2 — Direct answer

> Job and project tracking software is the record of what's been promised, scheduled,
> and completed — Jobber for dispatch-and-field-service businesses, Zoho Projects for
> a more general project shape. The value is in the connections: a completed job in
> Jobber should generate the invoice, not require someone to re-enter it, and a
> project's time tracking should feed what actually gets billed.

### 3 — What we run

- Platform selection — field-service dispatch vs. general project management is a real
  fork, and the wrong choice shows up fast.
- Configuration — job templates, scheduling, and offline behaviour for field crews
  (see [trades](/industries/trades) for the field-specific version of this problem).
- Integration with invoicing and accounting, so completed work becomes billed work
  automatically.

### 4 — FAQ

> **We're a trades business — is this the same as dispatch software?**
> Yes, for field-service businesses this _is_ the dispatch/job-tracking layer. See
> [trades](/industries/trades) for the field-specific detail (offline behaviour,
> device considerations, seasonal crews).

> **Does this replace our invoicing software?**
> No — it feeds it. See [invoicing software](/managed-it-services/invoicing-software)
> for that half.

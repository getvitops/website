### 2 — Direct answer

> CRM software means the system of record for who you talk to, what they've bought,
> and what happens next. Zoho Bigin, HubSpot and Jobber cover most small-business cases
> — Bigin and HubSpot for a general sales pipeline, Jobber where the work is
> field-service jobs rather than a sales cycle. We choose based on how your team
> actually works, configure it, and connect it to your accounting and marketing tools
> so a lead's status doesn't have to be retyped in three places.

### 3 — What we run

- Platform selection based on your actual sales or job-tracking motion.
- Configuration — pipelines, fields, automations that match how deals actually move.
- Integration with accounting and marketing tools, so nothing needs retyping.

### 4 — FAQ

> **We already have a CRM nobody uses. Can you fix that instead of replacing it?**
> Often, yes — a CRM nobody uses is usually a configuration problem, not a platform
> problem. We'll tell you honestly which one it is before recommending a switch.

> **Does this connect to our marketing campaigns?**
> Yes, when you're also on a digital marketing plan — leads route into the CRM and
> trigger follow-up automatically. See [digital marketing](/digital-marketing).

### 2 — Direct answer

> Invoicing software is what generates and tracks what you bill clients — Zoho Invoice
> for a general services business, Jobber where invoicing follows a job. The point of
> configuring it properly is that it feeds your bookkeeping directly: a paid invoice
> lands coded correctly instead of as a lump your bookkeeper has to unpick by hand.

### 3 — What we run

- Platform selection and setup, tied to how billing actually happens in your business.
- Integration with your bookkeeping software (see [bookkeeping and payroll
  software](/managed-it-services/bookkeeping-payroll-software)), so nothing gets
  retyped.
- Templates, payment terms, and automated reminders configured once, not per-invoice.

### 4 — FAQ

> **Does this replace our bookkeeper?**
> No — this is the software that feeds your books. Whoever does your bookkeeping
> (you or your accountant) still works from what it produces.

> **Can clients pay directly through the invoice?**
> Yes, where the platform supports it — online payment links are part of the setup.

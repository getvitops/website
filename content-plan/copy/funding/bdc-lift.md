**H1:** BDC LIFT — financing for AI adoption, not a grant.

**Direct answer (first paragraph):**
LIFT is a federal program from the Business Development Bank of Canada combining advisory
consulting with financing for businesses investing in advanced technology. **It is a loan,
not a grant** — a distinction frequently lost in third-party write-ups. **As of July 2026**
it runs two paths: digital transformation and AI, which requires **minimum $1 million** in
annual revenue and mandatory BDC advisory planning; and productivity and equipment, which
requires **minimum $5 million** and is limited to sectors including manufacturing,
transportation, construction, agriculture, wholesale, engineering services and extraction.
Both require that the technology or equipment be purchased from **Canadian suppliers**.
BDC does not publish a maximum amount; it depends on revenue, project scope and financial
strength.
[Source: BDC](https://www.bdc.ca/en/solutions/lift) · verified 2026-07-30

## Why this page leads with "not a grant"

Because a lot of pages don't, and the correction usually arrives at the worst possible
moment — after someone has built a budget around free money that turns out to be debt.

LIFT is a reasonable instrument for the right project. It is preferential-rate lending
paired with advisory work, and for a company financing a substantial AI or automation
programme it can make the numbers work. It is not comparable to
[DMAP](/funding/dmap) or [TDP](/funding/tdp), which are grants.

If you are choosing between them: the Ontario DCC programs are the first place to look,
and LIFT is worth considering when the project is larger than they can cover and you'd be
financing the balance regardless.

## The Canadian-supplier requirement

Worth naming plainly: LIFT requires that the technology come from Canadian providers.
**Vitops is a Canadian supplier**, so digital work delivered by us qualifies under that
condition. That isn't a reason to take financing you don't need — but if you're already
planning to, it removes a constraint that rules out a lot of vendors.

## FAQ

**Is LIFT the replacement for CDAP?**
Effectively, yes. The Canada Digital Adoption Program has ended, and LIFT is where BDC's
support for digital and AI adoption now sits — with the significant difference that CDAP
included a grant component and LIFT does not.

**What if our revenue is under $1 million?**
LIFT's AI path isn't available. The Ontario DCC programs have no revenue floor for
[DMAP](/funding/dmap), which is the better starting point at that size.

**Does the mandatory advisory phase cost extra?**
It's part of the engagement with BDC and priced by them. We're not involved in that
relationship and can't quote it.

**Can we use LIFT financing for the work Vitops does?**
Where it fits the program's scope and you qualify, yes — the Canadian-supplier requirement
is met. Whether taking on debt is the right call is a conversation for you and your
accountant, and we'd say so on a call rather than encourage it.

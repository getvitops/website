**H1:** TDP — up to $50,000 to build what your plan describes.

**Direct answer (first paragraph):**
The Technology Demonstration Program is an Ontario program administered by the Ontario
Centre of Innovation through the Digital Competence Centre. **As of July 2026** it provides
up to **$50,000** toward purchasing and implementing the technology identified in a
completed [DMAP](/funding/dmap). Two qualifiers are frequently omitted elsewhere: **a
completed DMAP project is required**, and the applicant needs a **minimum $750,000 in
annual revenue** in one of the last three tax years. It is listed **open until August 10,
2026**. Approval and amounts are decided by OCI, not Vitops.
[Source: Ontario Centre of Innovation](https://www.oc-innovation.ca/programs/digital-competence-centre/) · verified 2026-07-30

## What it actually covers

The build. Software, systems, integration and implementation work identified in your
DMAP — which in practice means this is the program that pays for a meaningful share of a
modernisation project rather than the thinking behind it.

## What stops people

**You cannot apply for this directly.** Every page advertising "up to $50,000 for Ontario
businesses" is describing something with a prerequisite in front of it. DMAP first. Plan
for two steps.

**The $750,000 revenue floor is real, and it excludes a lot of good businesses.** A
twelve-person firm can complete a DMAP, produce a sound plan, and be unable to fund it
through TDP. Know this before the DMAP, not after.

**There's a date on it.** Listed open until **August 10, 2026**. If TDP is part of your
plan, the DMAP in front of it has to happen with that in mind. This is the one genuine
piece of urgency in Ontario's digital funding landscape right now.

## FAQ

**How long between DMAP and TDP?**
Depends on the DMAP's completion and the intake window. With a deadline in front of it,
this is the reason to start the DMAP conversation now rather than next quarter.

**What if we're under $750,000 in revenue?**
Then TDP isn't available, and it's better to know that before you build a plan around it.
DMAP is still worth doing on its own, and [LIFT](/funding/bdc-lift) may be relevant if the
project is large enough — though that's financing, not a grant.

**Is the $50,000 matched?**
It's structured to match private investment in the technology. Treat it as a contribution
toward the project, not the whole of it.

**Does the deadline move?**
It has in the past, and it may again — but planning on that is not a strategy. We track
this monthly; if it changes, this page changes.

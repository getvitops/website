## Section 2 — Direct answer

Contractors have an unusual IT problem: the primary work happens away from any office, on
devices that get dropped, in places with unreliable signal, done by people who joined
recently and may be gone by spring. That combination breaks most standard small-business
software setups, and it's why generic advice about cloud tools tends not to survive
contact with a job site.

---

## Section 3 — What's actually different here

**Eyebrow:** What's different

**H2:** Four things a generic setup gets wrong.

**Signal is not a given.**
Anything that requires a live connection to record a job or complete a form will be filled
in later from memory, badly, or not at all. Field apps have to work offline and reconcile
when the device comes back into coverage. We select on that basis and test it.

**The device will be dropped.**
Tablets on a site get broken, left behind, and occasionally stolen. Every one of them needs
enrolment, encryption, and remote wipe, and the replacement needs to be productive the
same day — not after somebody reconfigures it from scratch.

**Seasonal headcount is a security problem.**
Crews scale up in spring and down in late autumn. Every one of those people got access to
something. If offboarding is manual, the accumulation of live accounts belonging to people
who left two seasons ago is invisible until it isn't.

**Safety paperwork has to survive an inspection.**
Hazard assessments, toolbox talks, incident reports, certification expiries. On paper these
get lost, filled in retroactively, or discovered missing during the one week you need them.
Digital forms with timestamps, and automatic warnings before a certification lapses, change
what an inspection or a COR audit feels like.

## Section 5 — FAQ

**H2:** Questions contractors ask us.

**Our guys aren't technical. Will they use any of this?**
That's the constraint we design against. If a form takes more than about a minute on a
phone with gloves half-off, it won't get filled in, and the software has failed rather
than the crew. Anything we roll out gets tested by the people who'll actually use it
before it goes live everywhere.

**Can you help us get COR-ready?**
We can put the documentation system in place — digital hazard assessments, training and
certification tracking with expiry warnings, incident reporting with a real audit trail —
which is usually the part that stalls a certification. The audit itself and the safety
program's content are your safety advisor's domain, not ours.

**What happens when a tablet goes missing on a site?**
We wipe it remotely, and the replacement enrols and configures itself when it's switched
on. The crew loses part of a day rather than a week, and nothing walks off with the
device.

**We hire twelve people in April and let eight go in November. Can you handle that?**
That's the normal shape of a trades account and it's the thing we'd fix first. Both
directions get run as a process rather than a scramble — including the part most people
skip, which is making sure the eight who left in November can't still get into anything
in February.

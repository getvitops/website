## Section 2 — Direct answer

_First body paragraph. Quotable standing alone — see `geo.md`._

Vitops is an Ottawa-based operations partner for Ontario small businesses. We run both
halves of the work a growing company needs handled — the digital presence it is seen
through, and the software, IT and back-office operations behind it — under one partner
rather than two vendors. The company was founded by Justin Lang and Alex Gagnon, who had
each spent years running one of those halves for the same kind of business.

## Section 3 — Mission

**Eyebrow:** Mission

**H2:** Run the work a small business can't justify hiring for.

**Body:**
Every growing business needs its software and devices, its books and payroll, and its
presence handled well. Almost none of them can justify a hire for one of those, let alone
three. Vitops exists to run all three under one partner, at a published price, for
businesses past the point where the admin is a real cost and short of the point where a
dedicated hire pays for itself.

**Origin story, retained beneath the mission statement (unchanged from the prior page):**
Working the same market from opposite ends, we kept hitting the same wall from different
sides. One of us would fix an onboarding process and then wait days on whoever handled IT
to create the accounts. The other would build a site that generated leads for a business
with no process to answer them. Neither problem sat inside either discipline — both sat in
the seam between them, which is exactly where nobody was accountable.

So each account has one of us on it as the owner: the person who makes the decisions, does
the work, and picks up when something breaks. What the second founder gives you is that the
other half never becomes somebody else's vendor.

---

## Section 4 — Vision

**Eyebrow:** Vision

**H2:** One operator on file, not two vendors and a seam.

**Body:**
The gap was never inside either discipline; it was between the vendors. We want the
ordinary state of an Ontario small business to be that the onboarding process, the systems
behind it and the site in front of it are one person's accountability — that nobody has to
project-manage their own suppliers to get a new hire a laptop and a payroll record in the
same week.

**Admission of the limit (`voice.md` requires this at least once per page):**
This doesn't scale indefinitely, so we take on a limited number of accounts. If we're at
capacity we'll say so rather than stretching and doing it badly.

## Section 5 — Values

**Eyebrow:** Values

**H2:** Four things we've decided, so you don't have to ask.

**We publish our prices.**
Per seat, on the [pricing page](/pricing). Not "contact us for a quote", which usually
means the number depends on what you look like you can pay.

**Month to month, 30 days' notice.**
No annual lock-in on either service. We'd rather earn it monthly than hold it
contractually.

**You own what we build.**
Websites on open standards, systems in your tenants, records in accounts with your name on
them. If you leave, you leave with everything.

**We'll tell you not to buy things.**
The first pass on a new account usually finds duplicate tools and licences nobody has
opened in months. Saying so is a better first month than selling you something else.

### Why those three credentials — see [`credentials.md`](../credentials.md)

Three of the nine credentials on LinkedIn have expired, and this page can't list an expired
certification as a current one. The site's persuasive strategy is verifiable honesty: the
funding pages publish the disqualifiers, every figure carries a date and a source. A
prospect who clicks a credential ID and finds "Expired Oct 2025" doesn't just discount the
badge — they discount the funding pages too.

Azure Administrator Associate is current but omitted from the sentence: Solutions Architect
Expert sits above it in the same track, so listing both reads as padding. It stays in
schema. PSM I and a 2014 Java certification are both still valid and both left off —
neither supports the pitch.

## Section 7 — Fit

**Eyebrow:** Fit

**H2:** Who this works for, and who it doesn't.

**Works well for:**
Ontario businesses roughly between 5 and 150 people, at the size where back-office admin
has become a real cost but a dedicated hire still can't be justified. Trades, brokerages,
professional firms and clinics are where we see that most.
[More on industries →](/industries)

**Not a good fit:**
Under about five people, where the admin isn't heavy enough yet. Enterprise brand
campaigns — a different discipline. Desk-side hardware repair; we manage warranties and
enrol replacements, but somebody local handles the device. And anyone wanting a vendor
rather than a partner.

**Caption:** Based in Ottawa, working throughout Ontario, and supporting businesses
elsewhere in Canada remotely.

## Section 8 — Scope

**Eyebrow:** Scope

**H2:** The boundaries, stated plainly.

We administer systems and operations. We're not your accountant, lawyer, benefits broker or
clinician — tax positions, legal opinions, plan design and clinical decisions stay with the
people qualified to make them. On funding, approval and amounts are decided by OCI, not by
us. And you remain the employer of record for your staff: this is not a PEO arrangement and
we don't take on employment liability.

## Schema

`Organization` on this page carries the `founder` array and the full `sameAs` list. This
is the entity-resolution page for the whole site — the identical `Organization` block goes
everywhere, and `sameAs` must be exhaustive and correct, since it's how an answer engine
decides two mentions of "Vitops" are the same company.

```json
{
  "@type": "Organization",
  "name": "Vitops",
  "url": "https://vitops.ca",
  "email": "hi@vitops.ca",
  "areaServed": ["Ottawa", "Ontario", "Canada"],
  "founder": [
    {
      "@type": "Person",
      "name": "Justin Lang",
      "sameAs": "https://www.linkedin.com/in/justinl-viabilityhr"
    },
    {
      "@type": "Person",
      "name": "Alex Gagnon",
      "sameAs": "https://www.linkedin.com/in/alex-gagnon-658108189/"
    }
  ],
  "sameAs": [
    "https://www.linkedin.com/company/getvitops",
    "https://www.instagram.com/getvitops",
    "https://www.tiktok.com/@getvitops",
    "https://www.youtube.com/@getvitops",
    "https://www.facebook.com/getvitops"
  ]
}
```

`alumniOf` is set on each `Person` for the PhD and the MCS. `hasCredential` goes on Alex's

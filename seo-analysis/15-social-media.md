# /social-media: SEO analysis

Status: proposal for review. Nothing here is applied to `seed/seed.json`.
Data: Semrush, Canada database, pulled 2026-11-26. Volumes are monthly searches in Canada.

## Role and verdict

Marketing sub-service. **Geo tier A**, and a quick win: the keywords are easy to rank for
(difficulty 12 to 14) even though volume is modest. The existing Ottawa tracking showed 20 a
month; the Canada-wide database shows 260 for the Ottawa phrase, so the real demand is larger
than the tracking suggests.

## Current state

- Title: `Social media | Vitops`. H1: "Show up in their feed, every week." (good).
- The model (you send photos and video, Vitops plans, writes and posts on up to 3 channels) is
  clear and a real selling point for busy owners.

## Recommended changes, highest impact first

1. **Title and meta (high).**
   - Title (42): `Social Media Management in Ottawa | Vitops`
   - Meta (144): `You send the photos. We plan the schedule, write the captions and post on up to 3 channels. Social media management for Ottawa small businesses.`
2. **Use "social media management" and "social media marketing" in headings (medium).** The page
   says "Show up in their feed"; good for voice, but the terms people search are missing.
3. **Show what a month looks like (medium).** A sample month (number of posts, channels, what the
   client approves) answers the question buyers have. Keep it true to the plan.
4. **Link to reviews and profiles (medium).** Posts feed the business profiles and the
   review-request flow.
5. **Structured data (low).** `Service` plus `BreadcrumbList`.

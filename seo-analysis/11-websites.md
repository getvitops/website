# /websites: SEO analysis

Status: proposal for review. Nothing here is applied to `seed/seed.json`.
Data: Semrush, Canada database, pulled 2026-11-26. Volumes are monthly searches in Canada.

## Role and verdict

Marketing sub-service. **Geo tier A:** Ottawa in the title and H1 or sub-heading. This is the
second-largest search opportunity after SEO, with two angles: new web design (competitive) and
website maintenance (large and much easier). The page already offers both ("keep what you have,
or start fresh"; hosting, updates and security included), so the work is mostly naming.

## What ranks now (web design ottawa)

Agency home and service pages (WebDesignOttawa, Webshark, MarketingBlendz, Ottawa Web Design,
1dea), directories (The Manifest, Clutch), a Reddit thread and a YouTube video. The agencies'
pages are short and direct, with the service and city in the title and headings. There is no
long guide in the top ten, so a clear page with the right wording can compete.

## Current state

- Title: `Websites | Vitops`. H1: "A fast website that brings in customers." (good).
- Kicker "Websites". "Ottawa" does not appear above the fold.
- Pricing: three website plans from $39 a month with a $500 setup fee.

## Recommended changes, highest impact first

1. **Title and meta (high).**
   - Title (48): `Web Design for Small Business in Ottawa | Vitops`
   - Meta (143): `Fast, search-ready websites for Ottawa small businesses, hosted and maintained for you. Keep your current site and speed it up, or start fresh.`
2. **Add a "Website maintenance and hosting" section with that exact heading (high).** The page
   says "we host it, update it and keep it secure". Naming it as a service targets
   `website maintenance services` (1,300, difficulty 19) without extra pages.
3. **Say the city and the audience above the fold (high).** Kicker:
   `Web design for small businesses in Ottawa`.
4. **Show price and setup together (medium).** "From $39 a month" is a strong draw, but the $500
   setup should sit next to it so the number does not read as misleading. This is also a trust
   point in a market where agencies hide prices.
5. **Link both ways (medium).** From `/digital-marketing` and `/seo-ai-search` using the anchor
   "Websites"; back from this page to both.
6. **Structured data (medium).** `Service` (area served Ottawa and Ontario) and `BreadcrumbList`.

## Current state

- Title: `Communication software | Vitops`. H1: "Chat and meetings, in one place your team
  actually uses." (good).
- Names Microsoft Teams, Google Chat and Meet, Zoho Cliq and Slack; covers team chat, video
  meetings, short video updates, shared inboxes and help desks, and frontline teams.

## Recommended changes, highest impact first

1. **Title and meta (medium).**
   - Title (59): `Slack, Microsoft Teams or Google Chat for Business | Vitops`
   - Meta (150): `Microsoft Teams, Slack, Google Chat or Zoho Cliq, set up to match the suite you already pay for and tied to the same logins as the rest of your tools.`
2. **Add a "Slack vs Microsoft Teams" heading with a short answer (medium).** Two or three
   sentences: when each fits, and why the suite you already pay for usually wins.
3. **Add a small decision table (medium).** Which suite you use (Microsoft 365, Google
   Workspace, Zoho) against the tool that comes with it.
4. **Link to productivity (medium).** The page and `/software-productivity` are two halves of
   the same decision; link both ways.
5. **Structured data (low).** `Service` plus `BreadcrumbList`.

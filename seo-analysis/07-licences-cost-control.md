# /licences-cost-control: SEO analysis

Status: proposal for review. Nothing here is applied to `seed/seed.json`.
Data: Semrush, Canada database, pulled 2026-11-26. Terms not listed returned no volume.

## Role and verdict

Managed software sub-service and the strongest cost story on the site. **Geo tier B.** Volumes are tiny
but the ad prices are very high ($41 to $48 a click), which tells you vendors value this
audience. A service business will not beat software vendors for these terms; the value is the
page's pull for readers who already arrive from the pillar.

the headings.

## Current state

- Title: `Licences and cost control | Vitops`. H1 "Pay for the software you use. Nothing more." is
  good.
- About 340 words. The sample bill (a 22-person business) is the best element: it is concrete.

## Recommended changes, highest impact first

1. **Title and meta (medium).**
   - Title (53): `Software Licence Management and Cost Control | Vitops`
   - Meta (149): `We track every software licence, cut the ones nobody uses and right-size your plans, so your bill matches your team. For small businesses in Ontario.`
2. **Keep the sample bill and add one real saving (high).** A sentence such as "a 22-person
   business found $X a month in unused seats" is more persuasive than any keyword. Only add it
   if it is true and you can share it.
3. **Mention Microsoft 365 by name (medium).** The commonest big licence line for a small
   business; it also matches `microsoft 365 license optimization`.
4. **Link to joining-and-leaving and the dashboard (medium).** Leavers' seats are the commonest
   waste, and the dashboard shows licences in use.
5. **Structured data (low).** `Service` plus `BreadcrumbList`.

# /business-profiles: SEO analysis

Status: proposal for review. Nothing here is applied to `seed/seed.json`.
Data: Semrush, Canada database, pulled 2026-11-26. Volumes are monthly searches in Canada.

## Role and verdict

Marketing sub-service. **Geo tier A.** The page covers Google, Bing and Apple profiles, and the
biggest single search is for Apple's product, `apple business connect` (1,300 a month). Most
of that is people looking for how to use it, not for a provider, so it is worth a short
how-it-works section rather than the main target.

## Current state

- Title: `Business profiles | Vitops`. H1: "Show up on the map, wherever people look." (good).
- Three profiles (Google, Bing, Apple), monthly posts and review replies.

## Recommended changes, highest impact first

1. **Title and meta (high).**
   - Title (57): `Google Business Profile Setup and Care in Ottawa | Vitops`
   - Meta (146): `We set up and look after your Google, Bing and Apple business profiles: correct hours, photos and replies to reviews. For Ottawa small businesses.`
2. **Add an "Apple Business Connect" section (medium).** Two short paragraphs on what it is and
   what you do for the client. It targets the 1,300 searches honestly.
3. **Say "Google Business Profile management" in a heading (medium).** The page says "business
   profiles"; searchers use "Google Business Profile".
4. **Link to reviews and SEO (medium).** Review replies connect to `/reviews-email`, and
   listings feed `/seo-ai-search`.
5. **Structured data (low).** `Service` plus `BreadcrumbList`.

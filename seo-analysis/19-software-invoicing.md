# /software-invoicing: SEO analysis

Status: proposal for review. Nothing here is applied to `seed/seed.json`.
Data: Semrush, Canada database, pulled 2026-11-27. See `17-software-pages-overview.md` first.

## Role and verdict

Software category page. **Geo tier B.** Smaller demand than CRM, but `quote and invoice
software` has a $30 click on only 70 searches, which means vendors pay a lot for a buyer. The
page's best angle is the whole path from quote to payment, which most invoice pages skip.

## Current state

  engagement letters, invoicing, invoicing from jobs and industry fit.

## Recommended changes, highest impact first

1. **Title and meta (high).**
   - Title (54): `Quote and Invoice Software for Small Business | Vitops`
   - Meta (141): `Ignition, Zoho Invoice, QuickBooks or Jobber, set up so quotes are signed online, deposits are paid upfront and invoices go out on their own.`
2. **Add a "which fits" table (high).** Four rows: Ignition for proposals and recurring billing,
   Zoho Invoice for simple invoices, QuickBooks for the books, Jobber for job-based billing.
3. **Lead with the quote-to-cash path (medium).** "From quote to paid" is the differentiator;
   repeat it as a heading ("Quote to payment, set up once").
4. **State the neutrality (medium).** Say the client owns the accounts, and disclose referral payments from QuickBooks and Jobber (see `27-referral-disclosure.md`).
5. **Link across (medium).** To `/software-bookkeeping-payroll` (the books these feed) and
   `/software-crm` (quotes start in the CRM).
6. **Structured data (low).** `Service` plus `BreadcrumbList`.

# /reviews-email: SEO analysis

Status: proposal for review. Nothing here is applied to `seed/seed.json`.
Data: Semrush, Canada database, pulled 2026-11-26. Volumes are monthly searches in Canada.

## Role and verdict

Marketing sub-service. **Geo tier B.** Two topics on one page: Google review requests and email
campaigns. Both have modest demand. Together they are a retention story ("keep past customers in
touch"), which is how the page should be framed. No Ottawa phrase has meaningful volume, so
the title stays geo-neutral.

- The review request attached to receipts and payment thank-yous is concrete and persuasive.

## Recommended changes, highest impact first

1. **Title and meta (medium).**
   - Title (43): `Google Reviews and Email Campaigns | Vitops`
     (The earlier brief's `... for Small Business | Vitops` is 62 characters and gets cut off.)
   - Meta (154): `We add a review request to every receipt and send a regular newsletter, so you get more Google reviews and repeat customers. For Ontario small businesses.`
2. **Use the words people search (medium).** "Get more Google reviews" in a heading and "email
   marketing" in the newsletter section.
3. **Explain consent for email (medium).** One line on how sign-ups are collected and that
   people can unsubscribe. This is a trust point and relevant to Canadian anti-spam law; the
   privacy and cookie pages should agree with it.
4. **Link to business profiles and social media (medium).** Reviews live on the Google profile.
5. **Structured data (low).** `Service` plus `BreadcrumbList`.

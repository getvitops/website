# /support-vendor-management: SEO analysis

Status: proposal for review. Nothing here is applied to `seed/seed.json`.
Data: Semrush, Canada database, pulled 2026-11-26. Terms not listed returned no volume.

## Role and verdict

Managed IT sub-service with the **best search opportunity of the seven**. **Geo tier A:** Ottawa
in the title and H1 or sub-heading. It takes the "IT support" terms the pillar hands off. The
page's current name ("Support and vendor management") says what you do but not what people
type.

## What ranks now (it support ottawa)

The results are mixed. Indeed, LinkedIn Jobs, Eluta, Workopolis and Glassdoor (job boards) fill
five of the ten spots, plus a City of Ottawa page. The providers on the page are Taleam Systems,
Firewall Technical, Convergence Networks and IT Department. Two takeaways:

- Half the page is job listings, so a clear, plain "IT support for small businesses in Ottawa"
  provider page has less competition than the volume suggests.
- Providers win with a short, direct page; there are no long guides in the top ten.

## Current state

- Title: `Support and vendor management | Vitops`. No "IT support" and no place.
- H1: "One number to call. We handle the rest." Good brand voice.
- About 340 words, a "Today vs With Vitops" comparison and four approach cards.

## Recommended changes, highest impact first

1. **Title and meta (high).**
   - Title (48): `IT Support in Ottawa for Small Business | Vitops`
   - Meta (117): `One number for IT support in Ottawa. We fix most problems ourselves and deal with your software vendors for the rest.`
2. **Change the kicker and lead to say "IT support" (high).** Kicker:
   `IT support for small businesses in Ottawa`. Lead: start with "When something breaks, call one
   number for IT support".
3. **Describe how support works (high).** Add two or three lines on how a person gets help
   (phone, email, the portal), a response commitment if there is one, and remote versus on-site.
   `remote it support` (260) and `it helpdesk services` (390) both ask that.
4. **Link up and sideways (medium).** Link to `/managed-it` ("Managed IT services"),
   `/backup-security` (restore requests) and `/dashboard` (open requests).
5. **Structured data (medium).** `Service` (areaServed Ottawa) plus `BreadcrumbList`.

## Cannibalization

Owns "IT support", "helpdesk", "remote support" and vendor management. `/managed-it` should not
use "IT support" as a target term; it links here for it.

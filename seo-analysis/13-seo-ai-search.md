# /seo-ai-search: SEO analysis

Status: proposal for review. Nothing here is applied to `seed/seed.json`.
Data: Semrush, Canada database, pulled 2026-11-26. Volumes are monthly searches in Canada.

## Role and verdict

Marketing sub-service and the **largest marketing search opportunity**. **Geo tier A.** It is
also the page that must practise what it sells: it should rank on Google and be easy for AI tools
to quote. The page is unusual because it covers both SEO and AI search; most Ottawa agencies sell
only one.

## What ranks now (seo services ottawa)

Local agency pages with a dedicated Ottawa SEO URL (FrontPage Online, FirstRank, GrowMe
Marketing, LocalSEOOttawa, Ottawa-SEO), a large agency (Thrive), directories (Clutch, GoodFirms)
and a LinkedIn company page. Ottawa-specific, service-named pages win. None of the titles
appear to combine SEO with AI search, which is the gap for this page.

## Current state

- Title: `SEO and AI search | Vitops`. H1: "Get found on Google and in AI answers." (good).
- The page explains the two ways people find you and that every page is reviewed before it
  goes live ("SEO shouldn't live in your inbox"), which is a strong trust point.

## Recommended changes, highest impact first

1. **Title and meta (high).**
   - Title (58): `SEO Services in Ottawa and AI Search Optimization | Vitops`
   - Meta (154): `We help Ottawa businesses get found on Google and in AI answers like ChatGPT. Every page is reviewed by you before it goes live. Start with a free review.`
2. **Name the service and city above the fold (high).** Kicker:
   `SEO services for small businesses in Ottawa`.
3. **Say "AI search optimization" in a heading (medium).** It is the term where the page is
   unusual. Use "generative engine optimization" and "AI SEO" once each in the body for readers
   who search those.
4. **Add a visible example or result (medium).** A real before-and-after (a ranking, a call from
   an AI answer, a calls increase) is worth more than extra keywords. Only add what is true.
5. **Add a free-review explainer (medium).** One short paragraph on what the free review
   includes. `seo audit` (70) searchers want exactly that.
6. **Link to websites and business profiles (medium).** The two are what make SEO work; link
   both ways.
7. **Structured data (low).** `Service` plus `BreadcrumbList`.

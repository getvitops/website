Status: proposal for review. Nothing here is applied to `seed/seed.json`.
Data: Semrush, Canada database, pulled 2026-11-26. Terms not listed returned no volume.

## Role and verdict

Managed IT sub-service. **Geo tier B.** Two demand signals: Microsoft 365 backup (high value,
hard to rank) and cybersecurity for Ottawa and small businesses (smaller, more winnable). The
page's "your cloud apps aren't a backup" argument is a good fit for the first.

## Current state

- Title: `Backup and security | Vitops`. H1 "Your data backed up. Your accounts locked down." is good.
- About 420 words, the longest of the IT service pages. Strong "Microsoft 365 on its own vs with
  Vitops backup" comparison.

## Recommended changes, highest impact first

1. **Title and meta (medium).**
   - Title (55): `Microsoft 365 Backup and Security for Business | Vitops`
     The earlier brief's `... for Small Business` runs 61 characters and is cut off.
   - Meta (155): `Daily backup of your Microsoft 365 and Google email and files, plus 2-step login and account lockdown. IT security for Ottawa and Ontario small businesses.`
2. **Add a security heading that names Ottawa (medium).** For example "Cybersecurity for
   Ottawa small businesses" over the accounts section. That is where `cybersecurity services
   ottawa` (difficulty 13) can be won.
3. **Say "Microsoft 365 backup" in the lead and a heading (medium).** The comparison block already
   does the work; the exact phrase is missing from headings.
4. **Link to joining-and-leaving and support (medium).** Account lockdown connects to
   offboarding, and a restore request goes through support.
5. **Cite the attack statistic (medium).** "Many attacks start with a stolen password or a fake
   email" needs a source and link; without one it reads as a claim.
6. **Structured data (low).** `Service` plus `BreadcrumbList`.

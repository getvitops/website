## Role and verdict

Managed software sub-service (renamed from `/ai-where-it-helps` to `/ai`; the old URL redirects).
**Geo tier B.** The broad term is the biggest in this group but also hard (difficulty 66). The
page will not rank for "ai for small business" soon; its value is being a clear, honest
explanation that AI answers can quote, and a conversion page for people who already trust you.

## Current state

## Recommended changes, highest impact first

1. **Title and meta (medium).**
   - Title (55): `AI for Small Business: Where It Actually Helps | Vitops`
   - Meta (140): `We find the repetitive tasks eating your team's week and set up AI to handle them, with permissions set first. For Ontario small businesses.`
2. **Keep the honesty and lead with it (high).** "Some tasks aren't worth it, and we'll say so"
   is the page's best line and what separates it from vendor pages. Move it higher.
3. **Name the tools people ask about (medium).** One sentence mentioning Microsoft Copilot and the
   assistants your clients already have. Only list tools you actually set up.
4. **Add a short definition and one concrete example (medium).** "AI automation for small
   business" in plain words, followed by one worked example with a real time saving if you have
   one.
5. **Link to the pillar and to backup and security (medium).** The "permissions first" promise
   connects to `/backup-security`; link both ways, and from `/managed-software`.
6. **Structured data (low).** `Service` plus `BreadcrumbList`.

Status: proposal for review. Nothing here is applied to `seed/seed.json`.
Data: Semrush, Canada database, pulled 2026-11-27. See `17-software-pages-overview.md` first.

## Role and verdict

Software category page. **Geo tier B.** Good commercial value for its size: `field service
management software` is 880 searches at a $31 click, and `field service software` 720 at $33.
The page covers two audiences (field crews and office project teams). The trades audience is the
one with the money behind the keywords and with a natural link to Jobber.

## Current state

- Title: `Job and project tracking software | Vitops`. H1: "What's promised, and what's actually
  done." (good).
- Names Jobber, Zoho FSM, Zoho Projects and monday.com, and covers field service and dispatch,
  project management, construction and renovation, sprints and software teams, and time
  tracking.

## Recommended changes, highest impact first

1. **Title and meta (high).**
   - Title (48): `Field Service and Job Tracking Software | Vitops`
   - Meta (146): `Jobber, Zoho FSM, Zoho Projects or monday.com, chosen for how work moves through your business and connected so a finished job becomes an invoice.`
2. **Split the two kinds of work under clear headings (high).** "Field service software for
   crews" and "Project management software for office teams". Different searches, different
   buyers.
3. **Lead the field service half (medium).** It has the higher volume and click value. Mention
   scheduling, dispatch, job notes on a phone and invoicing from the job.
4. **Add a "which fits" table (medium).** Four rows, with a column for who each suits.
5. **Link across (medium).** To `/software-crm` (leads become jobs), `/software-invoicing` (job
   to invoice) and `/connected-software` (the example on that page is a job).
6. **Structured data (low).** `Service` plus `BreadcrumbList`.

Status: proposal for review. Nothing here is applied to `seed/seed.json`.
Data: Semrush, Canada database, pulled 2026-11-27. See `17-software-pages-overview.md` first.

## Role and verdict

Software category page. **Geo tier B.** The best of the seven for search: the CRM terms have
useful volume, moderate difficulty and very high click prices ($13 to $20). Vendors and blog
listicles hold the top results, so the page wins on the Canadian, independent, set-up-for-you
angle.

## What ranks now (crm for small business)

Vendor product pages (Zendesk, Freshworks), vendor blog guides (Mailchimp, monday.com, Act,
Maximizer), a Canadian consultancy (Gestisoft), a directory (Capterra) and an Indigenous SME
listing. The only independent Canadian voice is Gestisoft, so there is room for another.

## Current state

- Names Zoho Bigin, HubSpot and Jobber as three common choices, with a capabilities list
  (pipelines, jobs, industry, forms, quotes, email follow-up).

## Recommended changes, highest impact first

1. **Title and meta (high).**
   - Title (50): `CRM Software for Small Business in Canada | Vitops`
   - Meta (155): `We help you choose, set up and connect a CRM like Zoho Bigin, HubSpot or Jobber, so every lead and customer is in one place. For Canadian small businesses.`
2. **Add a short "which CRM fits" table (high).** Three rows (Bigin, HubSpot, Jobber): best for,
   when it is a poor fit, how it connects to your other tools. It beats the description cards for
   both readers and AI answers.
3. **Add a "CRM for contractors and trades" paragraph (medium).** `crm for contractors` carries a
   $20 click; the Jobber section is already the answer, it only needs the wording.
4. **State the neutrality (medium).** "We don't resell any of these. You own the account." See
   the overview for the commission question.
5. **Link across (medium).** To `/software-job-tracking` (jobs), `/software-invoicing` (quotes)
   and `/connected-software`; up to `/software-selection-setup`.
6. **Structured data (low).** `Service` plus `BreadcrumbList`.
